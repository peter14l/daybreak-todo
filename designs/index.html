Compare the 3 Aesthetic Archetypes

Click on any card to switch the live interactive view above.

1. Windows 11 Fluent Luxe
Authentic Windows 11 Mica Alt translucency with refined charcoal surfaces (`#1c1c1f`), subtle 1px hairline borders, and native Fluent controls. Feels like a built-in Microsoft flagship utility without the Electron bloat.
2. Sunrise Warmth (M3E)
Rich espresso and morning amber tones (`#17131b` to `#251e2c`) with generous squircle curves (`18px`), tactile spring transitions, and warm editorial typography. Designed to make morning task planning calm and joyful.
3. Linear Obsidian
Ultra-sleek, developer-focused pitch monochrome (`#0d0e12`). Sharp 1px dividers, monospace micro-badges, zero rounded fluff, and hyper-dense information hierarchy for high-speed power users.
